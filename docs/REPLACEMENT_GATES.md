On 2026-10-17, a wireless LAN controller and lightweight access points (with central switching) landed as a documented reduced model. `WLC-PT` exposes two gigabitethernet ports with the management IP on the first one; `LAP-PT` sets `config.apMode: 'lightweight'` as the source of truth for AP behavior. The CAPWAP control plane (`src/simulator/capwap.ts`) runs discovery (~2 s) → Discovery Response → Join → fingerprint-driven Config Update with sequence acks → Run, plus echo keepalives (~5 s) and a ~15 s AP timeout, self-healing on both sides; it is modeled at layer 2 (ethertype `0x88B5`) on the wired broadcast domain, so APs need no IP. Central switching encapsulates radio frames to the controller, drops non-CAPWAP wired frames on lightweight APs, learns per-client tunnels, tunnels unicast to the owning AP, floods group frames to all registered APs, tunnels wired→wireless traffic, and leaves controller-addressed frames on the normal host path. The console is AireOS-lite (`show wlan summary`/`ap summary`/`client summary`/`interface summary`, `config wlan create/delete/enable/disable`), the device window adds `Config` (WLAN CRUD, read-only AP groups, management address) and `GUI` (`admin`/`admin` sign-in with Monitor/Clients/Management) tabs, the client site survey lists controller-advertised SSIDs, and a starter lab ships the whole path.
