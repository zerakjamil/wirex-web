On 2026-10-18, IPv4 BGP completed its reduced model with the TCP transport handshake and iBGP (superseding the 2026-10-17 eBGP-only paragraph). Routing-capable devices accept `router bgp <1-65535>` with `bgp router-id`, `neighbor <ip> remote-as` and `network <prefix> [mask]` (classful default) statements, their `no` variants, `no router bgp <AS>` and `clear ip bgp *`; a `remote-as` equal to the local AS now configures an iBGP neighbor, while differing-AS re-entry stays refused with an explicit message. Every session starts with the simulated TCP three-way handshake on port 179 (SYN retried every 5 s while Connect, SYN-ACK from a device that runs BGP with a matching neighbor statement, ACK + OPEN on receipt; a device that runs BGP without a neighbor statement answers RST and the peer returns to Idle and retries every 5 s), keepalives every 60 s against a 180 s hold, an OpenSent peer re-sends OPEN every 5 s, and the reduced FSM (Idle → Connect → OpenSent → OpenConfirm → Established) reaches Established on KEEPALIVE; a full re-open on OPEN-while-established matches the clear/reconverge workflow. UPDATEs carry the sender's full best-path set; local-AS is prepended only on eBGP advertisements (iBGP keeps the path unchanged, including the empty path of locally originated networks) and NEXT_HOP is rewritten for eBGP while relayed iBGP routes keep the learned next hop, accepted even outside the receiving subnet, at most 100 entries per chunk with the last marked `complete` (incomplete updates merge per prefix); entries containing the receiver's AS in the path are dropped (loop prevention), iBGP-learned paths are not relayed to other iBGP peers (no route reflection) but reach eBGP peers with prepending, and a remote-AS mismatch sends NOTIFICATION and resets the session. Best path is shortest AS_PATH → eBGP over iBGP → lowest neighbor address; eBGP routes install at distance 20 and iBGP routes at 200 (shown per route in `show ip route`), neither over connected subnets, and a `network` statement announces only while an operational connected subnet matches. eBGP is link-local (directly connected neighbor, TTL 1) and control traffic also rides operational GRE tunnels. CLI: `show ip bgp`, `show ip bgp summary` (including the Connect state), `show ip protocols`, `B` rows in `show ip route` with the per-route distance and the `router bgp` block in running-config. Verification: 10 new pure tests in `src/simulator/bgp.test.ts` (establishment with CLI output; a real PC-to-PC echo over the learned route; AS-mismatch NOTIFICATION with both peers' reasons; `clear ip bgp *` reconvergence; hold-expiry withdrawal on link loss; a three-router chain proving AS-path prepending, relay and loop prevention; the TCP handshake visible in the PDU stream; an iBGP pair without prepending at distance 200; a four-router no-reflection case that still prepends toward eBGP; a TCP RST refusal from a device that runs BGP without a neighbor statement) plus production-browser flow F13 (eBGP and iBGP neighbors from the CLI).
